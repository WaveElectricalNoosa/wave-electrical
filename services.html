Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you service the Noosa Hinterland?

Yes — we cover all of Noosa Shire including Cooroy, Pomona, Eumundi, Doonan and Tinbeerwah, as well as all coastal suburbs from Noosa Heads to Peregian Beach and beyond.

Are you a licensed electrician in Queensland?

Yes. Wave Electrical holds QLD Electrical Contractors Licence No. 92717 and ARC Refrigerant Handling Licence L138160. ABN 73 678 412 818. All work is fully licensed and insured.

Do you do same-day fault finding and repairs?

We do our best to respond same day to urgent electrical faults — particularly tripping safety switches, power outages and any fault that poses a safety risk. Call Steve directly on 0423 181 744.

Do you install EV chargers in Noosa?

Yes — we install dedicated EV charging circuits and wall chargers for all major brands including Tesla, Wallbox and Evnex, at both residential and commercial properties across Noosa and the Sunshine Coast.

Can you fully install a split system air conditioner, not just wire it?

Yes. Wave Electrical is ARC-certified (Licence L138160) for refrigerant handling, so we handle the complete installation — mounting, piping, gas and electrical. We specialise in split systems only, not ducted units.

Do you install and test smoke alarms?

Yes. We install and test hardwired smoke alarms to meet Queensland's smoke alarm legislation requirements for both owner-occupied and rental properties.

How do I get a quote?

Call Steve on 0423 181 744, email steve@waveelectricalnoosa.com.au, or use the online booking form. We'll get back to you promptly.

How long has Wave Electrical been operating?

Wave Electrical has been serving Noosa and the Sunshine Coast since 2018, building a reputation for reliability and quality work with over 200 five-star Google reviews.
