Home EV Charging — Done Properly

More Noosa homes are adding EVs, and the question of how to charge at home comes up almost every week for us now. The short answer: plug it into a standard power point and you'll charge slowly and put unnecessary stress on the circuit. A dedicated EV charging circuit — sized for the charger, properly protected, installed by a licensed electrician — is the correct way to do it.

We install EV charging setups for all makes of vehicle and all brands of home charger. We'll assess your switchboard, run a dedicated circuit from your board to the garage or carport, install the charging point or socket, and make sure it's protected correctly. If your board needs upgrading to handle the load, we do that too.

If you're building or renovating, now is the ideal time to future-proof for EV charging — running conduit during the build is much cheaper than doing it retrospectively.

Common Questions

EV Charger FAQ — Noosa

Can I just plug my EV into a normal power point?
Technically yes, but it's not ideal. Standard 10A power points charge slowly and were never designed for the sustained overnight load of EV charging. Over time this can stress the circuit and the socket. A dedicated circuit is safer, faster, and the correct long-term solution.
How long does an EV charger installation take?
Most residential EV charger installations take 2–4 hours. If the cable route is straightforward and the switchboard has capacity, it's usually done in a morning. If a switchboard upgrade is needed, add another hour or two.
What charger do you recommend for home use?
This depends on your vehicle. Most modern EVs work well with a 7.2kW (32A) Type 2 wall charger. We don't push one brand over another — we'll tell you what's compatible with your vehicle and what makes sense for your home. You can supply the charger or we can quote to supply and install.
Does my switchboard need upgrading for an EV charger?
Possibly. A 32A EV circuit needs a spare slot and sufficient capacity in your switchboard. We'll assess this before any work starts — if an upgrade is needed, we'll include it in the quote.
Do you work with Tesla, Hyundai, Kia, BYD and other makes?
Yes — the electrical circuit installation is the same regardless of the vehicle. The charger hardware varies by make. We can advise on compatibility and install whichever charger is appropriate for your vehicle.
