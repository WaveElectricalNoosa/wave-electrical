When Is It Time to Upgrade Your Switchboard?

If your home still has a ceramic fuse board — the type where you replace wire fuses when something trips — it's overdue for an upgrade. Old boards weren't designed for the electrical load of a modern home, and they lack the safety switch protection that Queensland now requires.

We upgrade switchboards all over Noosa every week. The job typically takes half a day: out with the old fuse board, in with a new panel fitted with circuit breakers and RCD safety switches. Every circuit is labelled clearly. The whole house is back up and running by lunch.

Most of our Noosa switchboard upgrades are booked for one of two reasons: the board keeps tripping and causing problems, or the homeowner is doing a renovation and their builder has told them the old board won't pass inspection. Either way, we make it straightforward.

Signs You Need an Upgrade

Does Your Switchboard Need Replacing?

Any one of these is reason enough to get it looked at.

🔌
Ceramic Fuse Board

If your board still has ceramic or porcelain fuses that you swap out when something trips, it's from a different era. Time for circuit breakers and safety switches.

⚡
Constantly Tripping

A board that trips regularly under normal household load is either undersized or faulty. We'll assess it and let you know what needs to happen.

🏗️
Planning a Renovation

Most renovations require a switchboard upgrade to pass inspection — especially if you're adding a kitchen, bathroom, AC units or additional circuits.

🔥
No Safety Switches

Safety switches (RCDs) are required on all power and lighting circuits in QLD. If your board doesn't have them, it doesn't meet current standards.

🏠
Selling or Renting

Buyers and property managers increasingly check for a compliant switchboard. Upgrading before sale or listing can avoid delays and negotiations.

🔋
Adding Solar or EV Charging

Solar inverters and EV chargers require dedicated circuits. If your board is already at capacity, an upgrade is part of the installation.

What's Involved

What We Do in a Switchboard Upgrade

Every switchboard upgrade is slightly different, but the process is consistent. Here's what you can expect when Wave Electrical upgrades your Noosa switchboard:

  1. Quote and planning — We look at your current board, assess your circuits, and tell you exactly what's needed. No surprises.
  2. Isolation and removal — The power goes off at the meter, the old board is removed safely and correctly.
  3. New panel installation — A new DIN rail panel is mounted, sized correctly for your home and any planned additions.
  4. Circuit breakers and RCDs — Every circuit gets a properly rated breaker. Safety switches (RCDs) protect all power and lighting circuits as required by QLD standards.
  5. Termination and labelling — Every wire is terminated correctly and every circuit is labelled clearly. No guessing which switch controls what.
  6. Test and certify — We test every circuit, issue a Certificate of Test, and restore power. You're done.

The job typically takes 3–5 hours for a standard home. We clean up after ourselves and leave the board tidy and accessible.

Switchboard Upgrades Include

  • Remove old fuse board / ceramic fuse box
  • Supply and install new switchboard enclosure
  • Rated circuit breakers on every circuit
  • RCD safety switches (power + lighting circuits)
  • Surge protection (on request)
  • Clear circuit labelling
  • Test and Certificate of Test
  • Additional circuits added where needed
  • Upgrade to 3-phase where required

Common Questions

Switchboard Upgrade FAQ

How much does a switchboard upgrade cost in Noosa?
Most standard residential switchboard upgrades in Noosa are in the range of $800–$1,800 depending on the size of the board, the number of circuits, and whether additional circuits need to be added. We'll give you a clear quote before any work starts — no surprises.
Do I need to upgrade my switchboard before a kitchen or bathroom renovation?
Usually yes. Most building inspectors will require a compliant switchboard as part of the renovation sign-off — especially for kitchens and bathrooms where dedicated circuits and safety switches are required. We often work alongside your builder to coordinate the timing so it doesn't hold up the job.
How long does a switchboard upgrade take?
A standard home switchboard upgrade typically takes 3–5 hours. Larger homes or boards with more circuits may take longer. We'll have your power back on the same day — usually well before the end of the working day.
What's the difference between a safety switch and a circuit breaker?
A circuit breaker protects the wiring from overload — it trips if too much current flows through a circuit. A safety switch (RCD) protects people — it detects a current leak (like electricity flowing through someone's body) and cuts power in milliseconds. Both are required in modern Queensland switchboards.
Can you add extra circuits to the new switchboard?
Yes — and this is often the best time to do it. If you're planning to add an EV charger, a second split system, or a home office circuit, we can add the extra breakers while the board is open. Much cheaper than coming back later.
