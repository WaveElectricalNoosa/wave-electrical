Common Faults

Electrical Problems We Diagnose & Fix

⚡
Safety Switch Tripping

A safety switch that keeps tripping — repeatedly or under load — points to a fault somewhere in the circuit. We find it and fix it rather than just resetting it.

🔌
Dead Power Points or Circuits

Power points or whole circuits with no power. Often a tripped breaker, faulty connection or damaged cable — we track it down to the source.

💡
Flickering Lights

Persistent flickering is usually a loose connection or failing component. Left alone it can cause overheating or arcing — worth getting sorted.

🔥
Hot or Burning Outlets

Warm power points, discolouration or a burning smell are serious warning signs. Stop using the outlet and call us — it needs replacing, not ignoring.

After flooding, leaks or condensation near wiring, we assess the damage and make the area electrically safe before anything is reconnected.

⚙️
Intermittent Faults

Faults that come and go are often the hardest to find. We have the test equipment and experience to track down intermittent wiring problems in older Noosa homes.

How We Work

How We Approach Fault Finding

A methodical approach saves time and gets the right answer. Here's what a typical fault finding visit looks like:

  1. Talk it through — When did it start? What were you doing? What happens when it trips? Context helps narrow the search significantly.
  2. Visual inspection — Check the switchboard, outlets, fittings and any visible wiring for obvious damage, overheating or water ingress.
  3. Circuit isolation — Narrow down which circuit is affected by isolating sections and testing with meters.
  4. Appliance testing — Many nuisance trips are caused by faulty appliances. We test plug-in equipment systematically to rule them out.
  5. Fix and test — Once the fault is found, we repair it and test the circuit thoroughly before we leave.

In most cases, the fault is found and fixed in a single visit. If the problem turns out to be more complex — damaged underground cable, widespread wiring degradation — we'll give you a clear quote before proceeding.

Common Questions

Fault Finding FAQ — Noosa

What causes a safety switch to keep tripping?
The most common causes are a faulty appliance on the circuit, damaged wiring (especially in older homes), moisture in a light fitting or power point, or a failing safety switch itself. We narrow it down by isolating circuits and testing appliances. In most cases we find and fix the cause in a single visit.
Is it safe to keep resetting a tripping safety switch?
If it trips once and stays on, unplugging the last appliance you added is reasonable. If it keeps tripping — especially with nothing plugged in — stop resetting it and call us. Repeated trips often mean a wiring fault that can cause overheating or arcing if ignored.
I've lost power to one room — is this a fault or a circuit breaker?
Check your switchboard first. If a breaker has tripped (switch is in the middle or off position), reset it. If it trips again straight away, there's a fault on that circuit. If the breaker looks fine but there's still no power, the issue may be a break in the wiring — call us.
How much does fault finding cost?
We charge a call-out fee plus time. Most faults are found within an hour. We'll give you a clear picture of any further repair costs before we proceed. No surprise bills.
Do you fix the fault in the same visit?
In most cases yes — we carry common parts and materials. If the repair requires parts we don't have on the van (e.g. a specific fitting), we'll advise the fastest way to get it sorted.
