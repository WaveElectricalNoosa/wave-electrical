About Wave

Built on the Sunshine Coast.
Built on Trust.

Wave Electrical was started by Steve, a licensed electrician who traded the cold winters of Minnesota for the warm shores of Noosa — and never looked back. After more than a decade in the trade and nearly 10 years calling Noosa home, Steve built Wave Electrical around the belief that every customer deserves an electrician who actually shows up, does the job right, and is honest about the work.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into a team of four — two qualified electricians and two apprentices — taking on everything from small home repairs to full commercial fit-outs across Noosa and the Sunshine Coast.
