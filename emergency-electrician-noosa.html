Electrical Safety

What to Do (and Not Do) Before We Arrive

While you're waiting for us — or deciding whether to call us — here's what we'd suggest:

  • Don't try to fix it yourself. Electrical work on mains circuits in Queensland must be done by a licensed electrician. This isn't red tape — it's genuinely dangerous work.
  • Turn off the affected circuit at the switchboard if it's safe to do so.
  • Unplug appliances from affected power points while you wait.
  • Don't use water near any suspected electrical fault — including switching off any circuit that runs through a wet area.
  • If there's a burning smell, evacuate and call 000. Better to overreact than not.

Common Questions

Urgent Electrical FAQ

Do you do after-hours callouts?
Call Steve directly on 0423 181 744. We do our best to help with urgent electrical issues — being local to Sunrise Beach means we're often available when bigger companies aren't. We can't promise same-day attendance for every request, but for genuine problems we make every effort.
My safety switch keeps tripping — is this an emergency?
It depends. If it trips once and stays on, it might be a faulty appliance — try unplugging everything and resetting it one circuit at a time. If it trips repeatedly even with nothing plugged in, or if it won't stay on at all, there's a wiring fault that needs diagnosing. Call us.
I've lost power to part of my house — is it my problem or the network's?
Check your switchboard first — if a circuit breaker or safety switch has tripped, it's likely an internal fault (call us). If your whole street is out or your meter box looks undisturbed, it may be a network fault — call Energex on 13 62 62. If you're unsure, call us and we'll help you figure it out.
There's a burning smell near a power point — what do I do?
Stop using that power point immediately. If it's safe, turn off the circuit at the switchboard. If the smell is strong or you can see discolouration or smoke, evacuate and call 000. Once it's safe, call us — a burning power point is a fault that needs replacing, not resetting.
