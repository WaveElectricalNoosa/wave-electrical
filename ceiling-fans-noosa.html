Keep Your Noosa Home Cool Year Round

In Noosa's climate, a ceiling fan is one of the most cost-effective things you can add to a bedroom or living area. Running a ceiling fan costs a fraction of what an air conditioner uses, and a good quality fan in the right size for the room will keep you comfortable on most summer nights.

Installing a ceiling fan isn't complicated, but there are a few things that go wrong when it's not done properly. The fan wobbles because it's not balanced or not hung level. It hums because it's on an incompatible speed controller. Or it's sized wrong for the room and barely moves the air. We've been hanging fans all over Noosa for years — we know what works.

We can supply the fan or install one you've already bought. Either way, it's hung level, balanced, and quiet. Controls wired in neatly. We test it before we leave.

Common Questions

Ceiling Fan FAQ — Noosa

Do I need a licensed electrician to install a ceiling fan in Queensland?
Yes — in Queensland, all ceiling fan installations involving electrical connections must be carried out by a licensed electrician. This includes connecting to existing wiring or adding new wiring. It's not optional, and unlicensed work voids your home insurance.
My ceiling fan wobbles — is it worth fixing or should I replace it?
Wobble is usually caused by a blade that's not balanced, a faulty bracket, or a fan that's too large for the mounting point. We can assess whether it's fixable or whether replacement is the better call. Often a blade balance kit sorts it — sometimes the fan's just had its time.
Can you install a ceiling fan where there's no existing wiring?
Yes — we run a new circuit from your switchboard to the ceiling. It's more involved than replacing an existing fan, and usually takes a half day rather than an hour, but it's very achievable in most homes.
Can you install fans with a light kit?
Absolutely — many fans come with light kits or can have them added. We install the full unit including light kit, and wire the light and fan speed separately if that's what you want.
How much does ceiling fan installation cost in Noosa?
A straightforward replacement or install onto existing wiring typically costs around $150–$250 per fan depending on the job. New circuit work costs more. Call Steve for a quick quote — most of the time we can give you a ballpark number in a two-minute phone call.
