Common Questions

Commercial Electrical FAQ — Noosa

Do you work on weekends or outside business hours for commercial fit-outs?
Where a project needs to be done without disrupting trading, we can discuss out-of-hours scheduling. Contact Steve to talk through what works for your situation.
Are your electrical installations certified?
Yes. All notifiable electrical work in QLD must be certified by a licensed electrical contractor. We issue a Certificate of Compliance for all work that requires one, and all work is inspected and tested before we leave.
Can you help with planning the electrical layout for a new tenancy fit-out?
Absolutely. We're happy to work through the electrical requirements early in a fit-out plan — power layouts, lighting design, switchboard capacity, three-phase requirements. Getting electrical planned early avoids costly changes later.
Do you work with builders on commercial renovations?
Yes, we regularly work alongside builders and project managers on commercial renovations. We're experienced at coordinating with other trades and working to construction schedules.
