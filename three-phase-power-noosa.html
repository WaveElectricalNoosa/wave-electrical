Single Phase vs Three Phase

When You Need Three Phase Power

Most Noosa homes run on single phase power — a standard 230V supply that handles everyday appliances comfortably. But there are situations where single phase reaches its limits: large commercial kitchens, big multi-split air conditioning systems, workshops with heavy machinery, EV fast chargers, and some large solar inverter setups.

Three phase power delivers 415V across three separate circuits, spreading the load and providing significantly more capacity. It also allows equipment to run more efficiently and start up more reliably under load — particularly relevant for motor-driven equipment and commercial refrigeration.

If Energex has three phase supply available in your street (common in Noosaville, Tewantin and other areas), we can organise the upgrade. If not, there are alternative solutions for some scenarios. Call Steve to talk through what's right for your situation.

Common Questions

Three Phase Power FAQ — Noosa

How do I know if three phase power is available in my street?
Energex's network information can tell us what's available at your address. In most of Noosa's commercial areas and older residential streets, three phase is available. Call Steve and we can check for your address before you commit to anything.
How much does it cost to upgrade to three phase?
Cost depends on whether three phase is available at the street, the distance from the supply point to your switchboard, and what switchboard upgrades are needed. Typically the Energex connection fee plus our installation costs. Call for a site-specific quote.
How long does the upgrade take?
The electrical installation is usually done in a day. The Energex connection and any street network work is scheduled separately — this can add a few weeks depending on Energex workload and whether any network upgrades are needed.
Do I need three phase for a large solar system?
Not necessarily. Many large solar systems work on single phase. Three phase solar inverters are useful when you want to balance export across phases — particularly in commercial buildings. We can advise on whether it's worth it for your situation.
Can I still use standard single phase appliances on a three phase supply?
Yes — three phase supply includes all three single phase circuits. Your standard power points and lighting still run on 230V single phase. Three phase just adds the capacity to run higher-powered equipment as well.
